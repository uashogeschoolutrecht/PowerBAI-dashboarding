# Dashboard storyline: What can I watch in the time I have?

## Idea

Students may have a gap between classes; employees may have unexpected free time
after asking AI to complete a task. This dashboard explores how movie lengths
differ across three streaming catalogs and helps people find titles that fit
the time they have.

## Audience and question

- **Audience:** Students between classes and employees with time freed up by an
  AI-completed task.
- **Question:** What can I watch in the time I have?

## Potential dashboard journey

1. Choose how much free time is available.
2. Compare how many movies fit on each platform.
3. Explore matching titles and their durations.

## An intriguing starting point

In the supplied historical catalogs, movies under 30 minutes account for:

| Platform | Movies under 30 minutes | Movies with a recorded duration | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Disney+ | 230 | 1,052 | 21.9% |
| Amazon Prime | 360 | 7,814 | 4.6% |
| Netflix | 124 | 6,128 | 2.0% |

These are shares of movies with a valid duration in minutes, not of all titles.
Three Netflix movies have no recorded duration. Some titles classified as movies
may be shorts or specials.

## Data and limitations

- Use the three existing CSV files in [data](./data/); no external data or title
  matching is required.
- These are historical catalogs, not evidence that titles are available today,
  in the Netherlands, or to a particular viewer.
- The data does not tell us what either audience watches or whether a title is
  suitable for teaching or work breaks.

This concept is for discussion and may change.
